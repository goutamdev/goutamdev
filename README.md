## 🔬 M.Sc. Research — Underwater Image Enhancement with DiT

**Problem:** Underwater images are severely degraded by light attenuation, wavelength-selective absorption (reds disappear first), scattering from suspended particles, and turbidity — making underwater vision unreliable for AUVs and scientific imaging.

**Approach:** Leveraging **Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM)** with **Diffusion Transformer (DiT)** attention to learn the conditional distribution of enhanced images from degraded observations.

**Datasets:** UIEB · RUIE · EUVP  
**Stack:** PyTorch · DiT · DDPM · OpenCV · CUDA
